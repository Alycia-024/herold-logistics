ABOUT HEROLD

Connecting Every Step of the Journey.

Herold Logistics Solutions is a logistics company focused on providing coordinated solutions for the movement, handling, storage and distribution of goods.

We recognise that successful logistics involves more than simply transporting goods from one location to another. It requires careful coordination, reliable processes and an understanding of how different stages of the supply chain work together.

Our logistics approach brings together transportation, freight services, warehousing, cargo handling, courier services and distribution. By connecting these activities, we aim to support businesses with the movement of their goods from collection points to their intended destinations.

Whether goods need to be transported, stored, handled or distributed, our focus is on creating organised logistics solutions that support business operations and keep supply chains moving.
